Simple Omelette Recipe

An essay and quick dish,perfect for any meal.this classic omelette combines beatten eggs cooked to perfection,optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetable or meat.

perparation time

  • Total: Aprpximately 10 minutes
  • perparation: 5miuntes
  • cooking: 5miuntes

Ingredient

  • 2-3 large egges
  • salt taste
  • pepper to taste
  • one table spoon of butter or oil
  • optional feeling:cheese,diced vegetable,cooked meats herhs

Instruction

  • Beat of eggs: In a bowl,beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper untill they are well mixed. you can add a tablespoon ofwater or milk for a fluffier texture
  • Heat the pan: place a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and add butter or oil
  • Cook the omoelette: once the butter is melted bubbling,pour in the eggs.till the pan to ensure the eggs evenly coat the surface.
  • Adding Feelings(optional): the eggs begin at set to the edges but are stll slightly runny in the middle,sprinkle your chosen fillings over one half of the omoelette.
  • Fold and serve: As the omoelette continue to cook,carefully lift one edges and fold it over the fillings.let it Cook for another minutes, then slide it onto a plate.
  • Enjoy: Serve hot,with additional salt and pepper if needed.
